## What This Node Does

Harmonic pitch-following synthesizer with glitch effects. Detects the pitch of
input audio using librosa's probabilistic YIN (pYIN), generates a synthesized
tone following that pitch, then applies one of 6 glitch effects and mixes with
the dry signal.

**Node key:** `ComfySynthMimicGlitcher`
**Category:** `audio/effects`
**Inputs:** audio (AUDIO), synth_volume, delay_time, waveform_type,
            mimic_effect, sensitivity, dry_wet_mix
**Outputs:** audio (AUDIO)

**Glitch effects:** bake (tanh distortion), scramble (chunk shuffle),
fry (high-pass emphasis), glitch (random roll), stutter (100ms repeat),
tape_stop (slow+fast resample)

## Architecture

The node operates in time domain only. Pitch detection uses librosa.pyin (hop=512).
The synthesized tone is generated by phase accumulation (cumsum of pitch array).
All glitch effects operate on the synthesized feed, not the dry signal.
